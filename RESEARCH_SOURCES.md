# Expansion research notes (October 2026)

The first expansion added **331 distinct queries** across 18 existing categories. A follow-up added **66 more distinct queries** across seven categories, bringing the collection to **16,233 queries across 19 categories**. The queries and descriptions were independently composed from documented product terminology, API surfaces, configuration fields, and public resource formats. Other dork collections were used to identify gaps, **not copied into this repository**. No new category was needed. The CSV files are the complete collection; category READMEs provide selected examples and counts.

## Selection and limitations

- Candidates were checked against every repository CSV using the collection validator's normalized-query rule (case, quote typography and whitespace). Four already-present queries were omitted; two more candidates were removed during manual quality review. Risk labels describe **review priority**, not confirmed impact.
- `<target>` means a domain after `site:`; in `site:github.com "<target>"` or `site:gitlab.com "<target>"`, replace it with a relevant organization or project name. A public repository or indexed API reference is not evidence that access controls have failed.
- The 331 first-round additions and 66 follow-up additions were **not individually tested against live Google results**. Google may ignore punctuation in URL patterns, omit non-indexable files, or return partial results; extension-specific `filetype:` queries are leads, not guarantees that Google indexes those formats. Validate promising queries manually within an authorized scope.

All **66** follow-up candidates passed the normalized-query comparison against the pre-existing CSVs and each other. Their descriptions and risk levels distinguish published documentation and configuration from verified exposure. They were not run against live search results; some `inurl:` patterns or quoted punctuation may be weak Google filters.
